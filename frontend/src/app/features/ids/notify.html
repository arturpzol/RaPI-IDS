Alert notifications by e-mail

A panel nobody opens reports nothing. Alerts are collected for a few minutes and arrive as one message, because a sensor on a mirror port can raise hundreds an hour and a mailbox with one message per alert gets a filter rule within a day.

How loud

The two settings that decide whether these messages keep being read.
